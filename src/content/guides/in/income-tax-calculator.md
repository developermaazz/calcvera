## How to use this calculator

The results update as you type.

- **Gross salary:** your yearly salary before tax.
- **Other income:** interest, rent after deductions and other taxable income.
- **Your age:** only changes the old regime's tax-free limit.
- **Deductions and exemptions (old regime only):** the total you'd claim, such as 80C (PF, PPF, ELSS, life insurance), 80D, HRA exemption and home loan interest.

The results show which regime saves more and by how much, your tax under each, and your monthly take-home pay under the better one. Below the calculator, the table compares every step of both calculations.

## Example: a ₹15 lakh salary

Say you earn ₹15,00,000 a year and would claim ₹2,00,000 of deductions under the old regime.

**New regime:**

- Taxable income: ₹15,00,000 − ₹75,000 standard deduction = ₹14,25,000
- Tax on slabs: ₹20,000 + ₹40,000 + 15% of ₹2,25,000 = ₹93,750
- Cess at 4%: ₹3,750, so total tax **₹97,500**

**Old regime:**

- Taxable income: ₹15,00,000 − ₹50,000 − ₹2,00,000 = ₹12,50,000
- Tax on slabs: ₹12,500 + ₹1,00,000 + 30% of ₹2,50,000 = ₹1,87,500
- Cess at 4%: ₹7,500, so total tax ₹1,95,000

The new regime saves **₹97,500**, leaving ₹1,16,875 a month in take-home pay (before PF and professional tax).

With large deductions, the old regime can come out ahead, as in the last row.

## How it's calculated

**Taxable income** = salary − standard deduction + other income − (old regime only) your deductions.

**New regime (section 202):** nil to ₹4 lakh; 5% to ₹8 lakh; 10% to ₹12 lakh; 15% to ₹16 lakh; 20% to ₹20 lakh; 25% to ₹24 lakh; 30% above. Standard deduction ₹75,000. Rebate (section 156) up to ₹60,000 if taxable income is ₹12 lakh or less, with marginal relief just above.

**Old regime:** nil to ₹2.5 lakh (₹3 lakh from 60, ₹5 lakh from 80); 5% to ₹5 lakh; 20% to ₹10 lakh; 30% above. Standard deduction ₹50,000. Rebate up to ₹12,500 if taxable income is ₹5 lakh or less.

**Surcharge** on high incomes, with marginal relief, then **health and education cess** of 4% on tax and surcharge.

The calculator assumes a resident individual. It doesn't include professional tax, income taxed at special rates (such as capital gains or lottery winnings), or employer NPS contributions. Figures are calculated at full precision and rounded for display.

## Tips for choosing a regime

1. **Add up your deductions first.** The old regime only wins if your deductions and exemptions are large.
2. **Remember the new regime is the default.** If you want the old regime, choose it when you file (salaried employees can also tell their employer).
3. **Check the ₹12 lakh mark.** Under the new regime, a salary up to ₹12,75,000 pays no tax.
4. **Don't invest only for tax.** Choose investments for their own merits, then see which regime suits you.
5. **Keep proofs.** HRA, 80C and 80D claims under the old regime need receipts and statements.
6. **Plan advance tax if you have other income.** Large interest or rental income may need advance tax payments during the year.
