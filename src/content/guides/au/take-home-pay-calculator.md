## How to use this calculator

The results update as you type.

- **Your pay:** your yearly salary before tax. Tick the box if it's a package that includes super. Add any salary sacrifice to super, and choose whether to see your pay weekly, fortnightly, monthly or yearly.
- **Loans and health cover:** tick the boxes if you have a HELP or other study loan, or if you don't have private hospital cover.

The results show your take-home pay, income tax, Medicare levy and super. Below the calculator, the chart shows where your salary goes, and the table gives every figure per year, month, fortnight and week.

## Example: a $90,000 salary

Say you earn $90,000 a year plus super, with no study loan and private hospital cover.

- **Income tax:** $17,520. You pay 15% on income from $18,200 to $45,000, and 30% on the rest.
- **Medicare levy:** 2%, or $1,800.
- **Take-home pay:** **$70,680 a year**, which is $5,890 a month, $2,718.46 a fortnight or $1,359.23 a week.
- **Super:** your employer pays 12%, or $10,800, on top.

With a HELP debt, you'd also repay $3,070.80, leaving $67,609.20 ($2,600.35 a fortnight).

If you salary sacrifice $10,000 into super, your taxable income falls to $80,000. Tax drops to $14,520 and the Medicare levy to $1,600, so your take-home pay is $63,880. That's $6,800 less in your pocket, but $10,000 more in super.

## How it's calculated

**Income tax (2026–27, residents):** nil up to $18,200; 15% to $45,000; 30% to $135,000; 37% to $190,000; 45% above. The low income tax offset (up to $700) reduces tax for incomes under $66,667.

**Medicare levy:** 2% of taxable income. On low incomes it's reduced: the calculator uses the ATO's 2025–26 low-income thresholds ($28,011 to $35,013 for singles) until the 2026–27 figures are published.

**Medicare levy surcharge:** without private hospital cover, 1%, 1.25% or 1.5% of taxable income, based on singles' income thresholds of $105,000, $123,000 and $164,000. Income for surcharge purposes includes salary sacrificed to super.

**HELP repayments:** 15% of repayment income over $69,528; $9,028 plus 17% over $129,717; or 10% of all repayment income from $186,051.

**Super:** 12% of your salary, up to the maximum contribution base of $270,830 a year. Weekly and fortnightly figures are the yearly amount ÷ 52 or ÷ 26, so your payslip may differ slightly from your employer's withholding.

The calculator assumes you're an Australian resident for tax, claim the tax-free threshold from one job, and have no other income or deductions. Figures are calculated at full precision and rounded for display.

## Tips for your pay

1. **Check your payslip against the estimate.** Employers withhold using ATO tax tables, so small differences are normal. Large ones may mean a wrong tax file number declaration.
2. **Claim the tax-free threshold from one employer only.** With two jobs, the second usually withholds more.
3. **Compare the surcharge with hospital cover.** If you're above the surcharge threshold, an appropriate hospital policy may cost less than the surcharge.
4. **Plan for HELP repayments.** They start once your repayment income passes the threshold, even if your salary sacrifice brings your taxable income below it.
5. **Watch the concessional cap.** Employer super and salary sacrifice together count towards the $32,500 cap.
6. **Lodge your tax return.** Deductions and offsets not included here can change your final tax.
