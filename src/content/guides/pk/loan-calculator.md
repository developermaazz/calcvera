## How to use this calculator

The results update as you type.

- **Loan amount:** how much you'll borrow.
- **Interest rate (APR):** the yearly markup rate your bank quotes.
- **Loan term:** in years or months.
- **Extra payment:** optional; an extra amount you'll pay each month.

The results show your monthly instalment, the total markup, the total you'll repay and the payoff date. Below the calculator, the chart shows principal and markup each year, and the schedule gives every payment.

## Example: Rs 1 million over 3 years

Say you borrow Rs 1,000,000 at 16% a year for 3 years.

- **Monthly instalment:** **Rs 35,157.03** for 36 months.
- **Total markup:** Rs 265,653.19.
- **Total repaid:** Rs 1,265,653.19.

## How it's calculated

The instalment comes from the standard loan formula:

**M = P × r ÷ (1 − (1 + r)^−n)**

- **P** is the loan amount, **n** the number of monthly instalments and **r** the monthly rate: yearly rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100. At 16%, r is about 1.333% a month.
- Each month, markup = balance × r. The rest of the instalment reduces the balance.
- Extra payments come straight off the balance, so later markup is lower and the loan ends sooner.
- The last instalment is adjusted so the balance ends at exactly 0.

The calculator assumes a fixed rate for the whole term. If your loan is linked to KIBOR, the rate and instalment can change. Figures are calculated at full precision and rounded for display.

## Tips for your loan

1. **Compare the total cost.** A lower instalment over a longer term usually costs more in markup.
2. **Ask how the rate is set.** Check whether it's fixed or linked to KIBOR, and how often it resets.
3. **Check all charges.** Processing fees, insurance and early repayment charges add to the cost.
4. **Pay extra when you can.** Even small extra payments reduce markup.
5. **Keep instalments affordable.** Leave room in your budget in case the rate rises.
6. **Consider Islamic financing if it suits you.** Many banks offer Shariah-compliant home and car financing with different structures; compare their total cost too.
