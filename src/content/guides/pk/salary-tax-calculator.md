## How to use this calculator

The results update as you type.

- **Monthly taxable salary:** your salary before tax each month, less any exempt allowances.

The results show your monthly income tax, the yearly total, your monthly take-home pay and the change from tax year 2026. Below the calculator, the chart splits your salary into take-home pay and tax, and the table compares tax year 2027 with tax year 2026.

## Example: Rs 200,000 a month

Say your taxable salary is Rs 200,000 a month, or Rs 2,400,000 a year.

- **Slab:** Rs 2.2 million to Rs 3.2 million, so tax is Rs 116,000 + 20% of the amount over Rs 2.2 million.
- **Tax for the year:** Rs 116,000 + 20% of Rs 200,000 = **Rs 156,000**, or Rs 13,000 a month.
- **Take-home:** Rs 187,000 a month.

In tax year 2026, the same salary paid Rs 162,000 (23% in that slab), so you pay Rs 6,000 less a year.

| Rs 50,000 | Rs 0 | Rs 50,000 | No change |

At Rs 1 million a month, the saving includes the removal of the 9% surcharge on salaried income over Rs 10 million a year.

## How it's calculated

**Yearly taxable salary** = monthly taxable salary × 12.

**Tax for tax year 2027** comes from FBR's salary schedule: in the slab your income falls in, tax = the slab's fixed amount + its rate × the amount over the slab's lower limit.

| Yearly taxable salary | Tax |
| --- | --- |
| Up to Rs 600,000 | 0% |
| Rs 600,000 to 1,200,000 | 1% of the amount over Rs 600,000 |
| Rs 1,200,000 to 2,200,000 | Rs 6,000 + 11% of the amount over Rs 1,200,000 |
| Rs 2,200,000 to 3,200,000 | Rs 116,000 + 20% of the amount over Rs 2,200,000 |
| Rs 3,200,000 to 4,100,000 | Rs 316,000 + 25% of the amount over Rs 3,200,000 |
| Rs 4,100,000 to 5,600,000 | Rs 541,000 + 29% of the amount over Rs 4,100,000 |
| Rs 5,600,000 to 7,000,000 | Rs 976,000 + 32% of the amount over Rs 5,600,000 |
| Over Rs 7,000,000 | Rs 1,424,000 + 35% of the amount over Rs 7,000,000 |

**Monthly tax** = yearly tax ÷ 12. The comparison uses tax year 2026's schedule, including its 9% surcharge on salaried income over Rs 10 million.

The calculator doesn't include tax credits (for example for donations, Zakat paid or voluntary pension contributions) or income other than salary. Figures are calculated at full precision and rounded for display.

## Tips for your salary tax

1. **Check your monthly deduction.** Compare your payslip with the calculator; employers base withholding on your expected yearly salary.
2. **File your return.** Filing keeps you on the Active Taxpayers List, which can lower withholding on other transactions.
3. **Claim tax credits you're entitled to.** Donations to approved institutions and contributions to approved pension funds can reduce your tax.
4. **Plan for a pay rise.** A raise can move part of your income into a higher slab, but only the amount above each limit is taxed at the higher rate.
5. **Keep salary certificates.** Ask your employer for a tax deduction certificate when you file.
6. **Report all income.** Rental, business or investment income is taxed separately and isn't included here.
